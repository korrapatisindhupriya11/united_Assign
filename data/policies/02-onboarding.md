# Onboarding

This onboarding policy is the checklist for new Harborline employees. People Operations runs the program with the hiring manager. Questions go to onboarding@harborline.example.

## Before day 1

People Operations sends the offer packet, tax and eligibility forms, and an equipment request after the employee signs. The manager confirms the start date, the team, and the name of a buddy. The buddy is a peer, not the manager, and supports the employee for the first 30 days.

## Onboarding steps

Follow these onboarding steps in order. Complete identity verification and payroll forms on or before day 1. Confirm the laptop has shipped or is ready for pickup. Attend the welcome session at 9:00 in the employee local time zone on day 1. Sign the handbook acknowledgment in AccessDesk on day 1. Finish security awareness training within 10 business days. Meet the buddy during week 1. Hold a goals conversation with the manager by day 30. Hold a probation or fit check-in by day 90 when the employment contract requires one.

## Access and equipment

The manager requests role-based access in AccessDesk before day 1. Default access is email, the handbook, the team channel, and the Time portal. Any extra system needs a named business reason. Harborline provides one laptop. Personal machines are not used for customer data.

## Who to contact

The manager owns team introductions and first assignments. People Operations owns forms, payroll setup, and the handbook acknowledgment. Missing access after day 2 should be raised in AccessDesk and copied to onboarding@harborline.example.
