# Performance Reviews

Harborline runs two written performance cycles each year, in March and September. People Operations owns the process. The manager owns the conversation with the employee.

## Review steps

The employee completes a self-review first. The manager writes the review second. The employee may invite up to 3 peers for optional feedback. Peer feedback is input, not a vote. The manager shares the written review in a live conversation.

## Ratings

The rating scale is Exceeds, Meets, Developing, and Off-track. A single Off-track rating does not start a performance plan by itself. A performance improvement plan is a separate process led by the manager together with People Operations. Monthly check-ins are recommended and do not create a formal rating.

## Disagreement

If an employee disagrees with a rating, they add a written comment within 10 business days and meet People Operations. The rating is not changed in silence. Compensation decisions are outside this policy. Pay questions go to the manager or to comp@harborline.example.
