# Benefits Overview

This page is a short map of benefits that People Operations administers. Country plans differ. The enrollment packet for the employment country controls when this overview and the packet disagree.

## Medical and retirement

Where a medical plan is offered, enroll within 30 days of the start date. A missed window waits for the next annual enrollment unless there is a qualifying life event. Where a retirement plan is offered, Harborline matches 4% of eligible pay after 90 days of employment. Personal medical records are not stored in this handbook and should not be pasted into chat.

## Learning stipend

A learning stipend of 1000 USD per calendar year can pay for courses and books tied to the role. Submit the expense in ExpenseDesk with a short note from the manager. Unused stipend does not carry into the next year.

## Pay is not listed here

Individual pay, pay bands, and executive compensation are not published in this handbook. That includes any CEO salary. Ask the manager or Compensation at comp@harborline.example. The assistant cannot look up what a person is paid.
