# Expenses and Travel

Finance owns expense and travel reimbursement. Submit company expenses in ExpenseDesk within 30 days of the spend. Questions go to finance-ops@harborline.example.

## Receipts and meals

A receipt is required for any expense over 25 USD. Travel meals are reimbursed up to 75 USD per person per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Itemize meals by date and city.

## Approval amounts

The manager approves an expense under 1000 USD. Finance approves an expense of 1000 USD or more. Splitting one purchase to stay under 1000 USD is not allowed. A travel expense over 1000 USD still goes through ExpenseDesk and waits for Finance after the manager reviews it.

## Booking travel

Book flights and hotels in TravelDesk at least 14 days before departure when the trip is already known. Personal upgrades, such as a higher fare class for comfort, are not charged to Harborline. Decline the upgrade or pay the difference personally.
