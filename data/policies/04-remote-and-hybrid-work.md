# Remote and Hybrid Work

This policy covers where Harborline employees may work. People Operations owns it. Contractors are not covered and follow their statement of work.

## Hybrid and remote roles

If an employee lives within 50 miles of a Harborline hub, the default is hybrid work: at least 2 days in that hub each month, scheduled with the team. A role is fully remote only when the job description says so. Any other fully remote arrangement needs written approval from the manager and People Operations.

## Working hours

Each team keeps a 4-hour overlap window chosen by the manager. Employees are reachable during that window on scheduled work days. Outside the window, respond on the next working day unless the team has an on-call rota.

## Work from another country

An employee may work from a country other than the employment country for up to 15 business days in a calendar year. A longer stay needs a mobility review by People Operations and Legal before the travel starts. A stay of three months is beyond 15 business days and always needs that review, because tax and employment law may change. Do not start the stay while the review is open.

## Equipment stipend

Harborline provides one laptop for remote and hybrid employees. An ergonomic stipend of 250 USD is available once per calendar year. Claim it in ExpenseDesk with a receipt. The stipend does not cover a second laptop.
