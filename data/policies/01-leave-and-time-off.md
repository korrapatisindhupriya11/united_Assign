# Leave and Time Off

This leave policy explains annual leave, sick leave, parental leave, bereavement, and unpaid leave for Harborline employees. People Operations owns this policy. It does not replace statutory leave. If local law gives a greater entitlement, local law applies. Ask People Operations for the country addendum at people-ops@harborline.example.

## Annual leave

Employees scheduled for 30 hours or more per week receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave is the paid time off benefit, also called PTO. Accrual is 1.5 days per month. Someone who joins mid-year accrues from the start month, prorated for that month. Employees scheduled for fewer than 30 hours accrue in proportion to a 40-hour week. Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry into the next year, and those carried days must be used by March 31. Annual leave is requested in the Harborline Time portal. The manager should approve or decline within 5 business days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave is separate from annual leave and is not paid out when employment ends. People Operations asks for a medical note only after 3 consecutive business days of sick leave. Do not send diagnoses or medical records to the team channel. Send any required note only through the Time portal.

## Parental leave

Harborline provides 16 weeks of paid parental leave for a birth, an adoption, or a foster placement. Every parent is eligible, regardless of gender. Use the leave within 12 months of the birth or placement. The leave may be split into two blocks when the manager agrees in writing. Notify People Operations at least 30 days before a planned start when the date is known. Statutory pay rules in the employment country still apply and are coordinated by People Operations.

## Bereavement and unpaid leave

Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for an immediate family member, meaning a spouse, partner, child, parent, or sibling. Extended family bereavement leave is 2 paid days for a grandparent, grandchild, or parent-in-law. Unpaid leave may last up to 30 consecutive calendar days and needs approval from the manager and People Operations. Request unpaid leave at least 15 business days ahead, except in an emergency.

## Blackout dates and questions

Finance employees do not take annual leave on the last 5 business days of a fiscal quarter unless the Finance director approves an exception. Other teams do not have a company-wide blackout. Questions about this leave policy go to the manager first and then to people-ops@harborline.example.
