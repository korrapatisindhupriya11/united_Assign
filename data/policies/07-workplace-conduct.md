# Workplace Conduct

Harborline expects employees to treat colleagues respectfully. Harassment and discrimination are prohibited. This conduct policy is an internal standard. It is not legal advice.

## How to report

Report a conduct concern to the manager, to People Operations, or to the ethics line at ethics@harborline.example. Anonymous reports are accepted on the ethics line. Include what happened, when it happened, and who was involved if you are comfortable doing so. You do not have to report to the person the concern is about.

## Retaliation and conflicts

Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited, including a change in hours, access, or rating that is meant to punish the report. People Operations reviews retaliation concerns separately from the original report. Disclose a conflict of interest before acting on it. A financial stake in a vendor is a conflict. Send the disclosure to people-ops@harborline.example.

## What this policy does not do

The ethics line does not take emergency safety calls. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Conduct investigations do not publish the reporter's identity to the wider company.
